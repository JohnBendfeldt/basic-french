Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 13, 2026

Plain English Summary: We believe in privacy by default. Basic French is a free educational tool. We only collect the minimal information needed to save your quiz progress and learning stats. We never sell your data, we do not run third-party advertising trackers, and you can delete your data at any time.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in two ways:

  • Guest Users (Unauthenticated): If you use the app without logging in, your quiz scores and word practice history are stored exclusively in your browser’s localStorage. No personal identification is collected or transmitted to our servers.
  • Registered Users (Authenticated via Google, Microsoft Entra ID, or GitHub): If you choose to sign in to sync progress across devices, we receive basic authentication details from your chosen OAuth provider (email address, display name, and optional profile avatar URL).
  • Learning Data: Quiz session scores, accuracy rates, and spaced-repetition mastery counts.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used strictly to save your quiz scores, generate your personalized "Words to Review" list, and sync your progress across devices. We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data with any third parties or advertisers.

3. Data Storage & Security

Authenticated learning data is stored in a PostgreSQL database managed via Supabase with strict Row Level Security (RLS) policies ensuring each user can only read, update, or delete their own records over HTTPS/TLS.

4. Your Rights & Data Deletion

Guest users can clear their local history anytime from the "My Stats" dialog or browser settings. Registered users may request complete account and cloud progress deletion by emailing dev@basicfrench.org.
